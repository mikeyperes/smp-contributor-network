What we look for

  • Original work that has not been published anywhere else.

  • Writing that is useful and easy to read for a broad audience.

How to pitch

  • Email [hws_site field="email"] with a short summary of your idea, why it matters now and why you are the right person to write it.
  • Include links to work you have published before, if you have any.
  • If you already have a draft, paste it into the email.
